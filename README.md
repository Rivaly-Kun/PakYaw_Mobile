# 🏆 Pakyaw App

**Pakyaw App** is a ride-hailing application designed to make commuting in **Ormoc City** more convenient, accessible, and affordable.  
Developed by **me and my team**, this project earned **1st Runner-Up (2nd Place)** and a **₱30,000** cash prize at the **2025 Ormoc Business Model & Prototype Competition**. 🎉💙

---

## 🚖 About
**Pakyaw App** aims to simplify transportation for Ormoc commuters through a reliable and user-friendly ride-hailing platform.  
The app bridges **technology and community needs**, promoting local innovation in urban mobility.
